StudyMe — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 September 2026

StudyMe is a personal self-tracking web app (a PWA). You create time-boxed “studies”, define metrics (mood, sleep time, habits, etc.) and log entries. This policy explains what data StudyMe handles and how.

What data we handle

StudyMe processes only the tracking data you enter: your studies, the metrics you define, and the entries you log (with their timestamps and optional notes). StudyMe does not ask for your name, does not use cookies for tracking, and contains no third-party advertising or analytics.

Where your data is stored

By default, all your data is stored locally in your browser (IndexedDB) on your own device. It never leaves your device unless you explicitly enable a sync or export feature described below.

Optional Google sync

If you choose to enable Google sync, StudyMe requests the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file scope. This scope is restricted to files that StudyMe itself creates — StudyMe stores a single spreadsheet with a copy of your tracking data in your own Google Drive, so you can access the same data across your devices. StudyMe cannot see or touch any other files in your Google Drive.

Your Google data is used solely to keep your own StudyMe data in sync. It is not sold, shared, or transferred to anyone, and it is not used for advertising or any purpose other than the sync feature you enabled. You can disconnect sync at any time; you may also delete the StudyMe spreadsheet from your Google Drive.

StudyMe’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Export & deletion

You can export all your data to a JSON file at any time, and delete studies, metrics, or entries directly in the app. Clearing the app’s browser storage removes all local data.
